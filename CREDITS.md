# Credits

The questions answered here are Questions 6.7, 6.8, 6.11 and 6.13 of R. Li, E. Mossel and B. Weiss, *Monotonicity,
topology, and convexity of recurrence in random walks*, Ann. Appl. Probab. 36 (2026), 4291-4310,
doi:10.1214/26-AAP2329 (arXiv:2403.04108v4). Their characterization of the recurrent set by zero-drift laws, and their
results on its closedness and on the transient set, are the starting point of the paper.

The paper also uses:

- The nerve theorem of K. Borsuk, in the form of Theorem 6 and Remark 7 of A. Björner, *Nerves, fibers and homotopy
  groups*, J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 102 (2003), 88-93, to identify the homotopy type of the recurrent set.
- A. Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, Cambridge University Press, 2002: Proposition 1A.2 and Exercise 1A.3 (graphs and
  their fundamental groups) and Theorem 1.7 (the fundamental group of the circle).

`check_lmw.py` uses only the Python standard library (exact rational arithmetic with `fractions`).
